Crafting

Two crafting systems. Enchanted items come from The Craftsman for a set of items plus Shards of Life. Superstitious items come from Draedon: you need a Catalyst, then you balance six elements with Reborn items.

The Craftsman

Enchanted recipes

Talk to The Craftsman, hand over every item on the list plus the Shards of Life, and the Enchanted item is yours. You also need to be at or above the listed life. Tick recipes to build a shopping list.

  1. 1Reach the life shown on the recipe
  2. 2Collect every listed item, with the counts shown
  3. 3Pay the Shards of Life at The Craftsman

Draedon

Superstitious recipes

Only after The Second Sacrifice, from life S+1000. Each Superstitious item needs its own Catalyst, then a set amount of six elements that you add by feeding Reborn items to Draedon.

  1. 1Click Draedon in the Sporest at S+1000 for the True Book of Knowledge
  2. 2Place the True Book and the Catalyst's 5 items, then rebirth: about a 1 in 3 chance of the Catalyst. The items stay.
  3. 3Take the Catalyst to Draedon and add Reborn items until every element reaches its target
  4. 4Forge it. The Catalyst and the Reborn items you added are used up.

Plan a forge

Draedon's forge planner

Works like the crafting screen: click a Reborn item to add one, right-click (or press −) to take one back. Every element with a target has to reach it. Elements with a target of 0 can be anything.

Added0 items

The crafting screen lists Reborn-tier items. Advanced Reborn and Slipstream items also carry elements on the wiki, so they're here for planning.
