Daily Tasks

Small goals you finish while you play, paid in uC. Every player gets a random set of daily and weekly tasks: each daily task pays 10 uC and each weekly task pays 50 uC.

The full list

Every task and its reward

Every task comes in a daily and a weekly version. The weekly one asks for more (usually five times as much) and pays 50 uC instead of 10. Press Track on the tasks you got to follow them in the tracker below.

    Keep count

    Task tracker

    Add the tasks the game gave you and count them off as you play. Your list is saved in this browser. Daily tasks clear themselves at the daily reset (8:00 PM New York time, when Daily Gifts refresh). Clear weekly tasks yourself when your week rolls over.
