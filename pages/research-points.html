How to get RP

Research crates

Crates drop from the sky all over the map. Walk into one to open it. You can also farm RP by burning ore in furnaces, which is covered further down.

RP per sky crate

What RP is for

Spending your RP

RP isn't spent when you unlock a shop tier. You just need to have earned that much. Type in your RP to see what's open to you.
