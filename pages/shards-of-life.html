Shards of Life

A currency you earn by skipping lives when you rebirth and by salvaging Reborn items. Spend it at The Craftsman's Reborn Shop, on Enchanted recipes and on platforms. Shards are Reborn-proof, but a sacrifice takes them.

Getting shards

Three ways to earn them

Shards per rebirth

Every suffix of cash above your rebirth price lets you skip one more life, up to 20. Skipping more also raises your chance of an Inferno Box.

Platforms & other buys

Platforms let you build on a higher floor. The shard ones come from the Premium Shop and their price climbs every time you buy one.

Hydraulic Platform cost

Starts at 100 shards and goes up by about 150 each time. The wiki notes the first few steps can be a bit smaller, so treat this as an estimate.
